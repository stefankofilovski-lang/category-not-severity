## S006 — Australian Automated Debt Assessment System Issued False Notices to Thousands

Historical reference: **high**. Author verification: **pending**.

It stopped using averaged income as the sole evidence of a debt in November 2019. The agency says money paid toward these debts was refunded and unpaid amounts were reduced to zero.

Source: [Services Australia](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/information-about-robodebt?context=60271). Official agency page retrieved by research browser on 2026-09-22; page updated 2023-09-28.

Review issue: The described averaging mechanism alone does not demonstrate a learned or inferential component.

## S013 — Police Departments Reported ShotSpotter as Unreliable and Wasteful

Historical reference: **high**. Author verification: **pending**.

Chicago's inspector general describes acoustic sensors that algorithmically flag possible gunshots and estimate their location. A human reviewer listens before an alert is sent to police. In the inspector general's January 2020–May 2021 data, 4,556 of 50,176 dispatched alerts were linked to a recorded gun-related criminal offense. That outcome measure is not a direct estimate of whether every detected sound was a gunshot.

Source: [Chicago Office of Inspector General, August 2021 report](https://igchicago.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Chicago-Police-Departments-Use-of-ShotSpotter-Technology.pdf). Official report pages 2–4 inspected by research browser on 2026-09-22.

Review issue: Identify a listed Annex III use, rather than treating every police tool as high-risk.
